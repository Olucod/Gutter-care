Why Gutter Maintenance Matters

Small maintenance jobs can help prevent bigger problems.

When gutters become blocked, rainwater may overflow onto walls, paths and the ground around your home. Keeping gutters clear helps rainwater drain away properly and makes it easier to spot visible issues before they get worse.
